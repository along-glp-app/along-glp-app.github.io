Your journal, on your device.

Privacy policy for Along 1.0. Effective 16 October 2026.

Along is published by Christine Cajilla. Contact: along.glp.1app@gmail.com.

What you record

Along is a personal journal. You may enter a name and preferences, medication and shot details, weight, appetite, symptoms, mood, energy, nourishment, cycle notes, personal markers, goals and free-text notes. Optional entries remain optional.

The app processes this information on your iPhone. It has no account, journal server, advertising, tracking or usage-analytics service. We do not receive your journal through the app.

Storage and deletion

Along uses iOS file protection for its local journal and excludes its journal and owned recovery/export files from automatic device backups. This is not cloud recovery. Removing Along or losing the phone can lose the journal unless you saved a usable backup.

Use You → Delete this journal to remove local entries, preferences, local recovery copies and the saved recovery code. Follow any error shown if deletion could not finish. Files you saved or shared outside Along remain with their recipients or storage providers.

Backups and exports you choose

You can save an encrypted backup through Files. It needs both the backup file and its separate recovery code to restore. Along uses Apple's CryptoKit encryption and keeps a recovery code in the device Keychain. We cannot recover a lost code or decrypt your backup for you.

CSV and PDF exports are readable files. Anyone receiving them can read their contents. Along removes its temporary export copies when sharing ends or at the next app start. A Files location or sharing destination you choose may be a cloud service and follows that provider's terms.

Questions and typed entries

Answers and summaries are written by app code using your records. On supported devices, Apple's on-device model may classify a question or suggest fixed draft choices. No network model is used. Typed drafts are not saved until you review them and tap Save.

Device features and external websites

Reminders are optional local notifications. You can switch them off in Along or change notification permission in iPhone Settings. Device-owner authentication may be requested before showing a recovery code; Along does not receive Face ID, Touch ID or passcode data.

Education and prescribing-label links open external websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices. This policy does not describe their data collection.

This website

This privacy and support website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security, as explained in its hosting information. We add no analytics scripts or contact forms to these pages. Along does not attach journal records when opening its privacy or support links. GitHub handles its hosting logs under its own privacy policy.

Support and Apple diagnostics

If you contact us, your email provider and ours process that correspondence. We use the message to answer your request. Please send reproduction steps, app/iOS versions and synthetic screenshots, never a journal, recovery code or health-record screenshot.

Apple may collect diagnostics or feedback under its own settings and terms, including through TestFlight, and may make reports available to the publisher. Along includes no third-party crash-reporting SDK. We use reports we receive to investigate app problems.

We retain support correspondence and diagnostic reports for 12 months. To request deletion of correspondence or reports we hold, contact the address above. We cannot remotely access or delete a journal on your phone or files you shared elsewhere.

Your choices and changes to this policy

You control what you enter, export and delete. Along works without an account or notification permission. The journal is intended for adults already prescribed medication; it does not provide medical advice or make treatment decisions.

If these practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. Any future service that sends journals off the device would require a separate product and privacy review before release.
