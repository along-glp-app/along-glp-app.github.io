A little help with Along.

Along 1.0 is a private, on-device journal for adults already prescribed a GLP-1 medication. Published by Christine Cajilla.

Contact support: along.glp.1app@gmail.com

Include your Along version, iPhone model, iOS version, the steps you took, what happened and what you expected. Use the sample journey for screenshots. Never send health entries, a journal file, a backup recovery code or a screenshot of a real journal.

Keeping a recoverable copy

In You → Backup & recovery, show the recovery code, keep it securely apart from the file, verify it and create an encrypted backup. Save both before changing phones or removing Along. The journal is excluded from automatic device backups.

To restore, choose your backup file and enter its matching code. Check the preview before confirming replacement. Restore replaces the journal; it does not merge records. Review and enable reminders again on the receiving device.

A previous local copy may help with a recent bad save, but cannot recover a lost phone. We cannot replace a lost recovery code or recover your journal from a server.

If saving or opening fails

Keep the app and phone, preserve any backup and code, and follow the error shown. Unlock the iPhone and try again when Along reports protected data is unavailable. Do not delete or reinstall Along as a troubleshooting step before securing a recoverable backup.

If the problem continues, contact support with the steps and error wording, without private journal contents.

Other common questions

No notification? Check You → Gentle reminders and notification permission in iPhone Settings. Reminders are optional; Along works without them.

Want estimated levels off? Use You → Personalise your companion → Show estimated levels. Estimates are not measurements or guidance about when or how much to inject.

Want to remove your records? Use You → Delete this journal. This removes Along's local records and recovery material; it cannot remove files you previously exported or shared.

Treatment questions

Along records personal history. It does not diagnose, recommend doses, interpret symptoms or decide whether treatment is working. Contact your prescribing professional about treatment. For an emergency, use your local emergency services; app support is not an emergency service.
